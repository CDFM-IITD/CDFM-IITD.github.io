## Ph.D. Students

We have several openings for Ph.D. students.
Applicants should have a strong academic background in materials science, chemistry, or physics.
Knowledge of computational materials science is a plus but not mandatory.
Candidates need to apply formally through the [IIT Delhi postgraduate admissions portal](https://home.iitd.ac.in/pg-admissions.php).

## M.Tech. / M.Sc. / B.Tech. Students

If you are studying at IIT Delhi and are interested in doing a project with the group, just drop us an email.
Some knowledge of materials computation is always a plus, but not mandatory.
You can take part in a wide range of problems that our group is working on (see [Research](../research)), and we also encourage you to bring your own ideas.

## Postdoctoral Researchers

Motivated postdoctoral candidates with experience in electronic structure, excited-state dynamics, or machine learning for materials are welcome to get in touch to discuss fellowship opportunities.
