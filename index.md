# Computational Design of Functional Materials

Welcome to the **Computational Design of Functional Materials (CDFM)** group at the [Indian Institute of Technology Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/).
We are part of the [Department of Materials Science and Engineering](https://mse.iitd.ac.in/) and the [Department of Chemistry](https://chemistry.iitd.ac.in/) (joint) at IIT Delhi, led by [Prof. Dibyajyoti Ghosh](members/dibyajyoti-ghosh).

Efficient functional materials are key to smart, cost-effective, and green technologies.
Finding the right material for a particular application is challenging and needs expertise from many fields.
Atomistic understanding–guided materials design, followed by experimental realization at the device scale, is a powerful strategy to push these technologies forward.

Our group uses high-level computational simulations to investigate and manipulate **energy conversion and transport processes** in optoelectronic, photocatalytic, and spintronic materials.
Building on this fundamental knowledge, we apply **statistical and machine learning models** for the in-silico design and discovery of efficient, environment-friendly materials for targeted applications.
