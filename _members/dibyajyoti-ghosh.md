Dibyajyoti is an Associate Professor in the [Department of Materials Science and Engineering](https://mse.iitd.ac.in/) and the [Department of Chemistry](https://chemistry.iitd.ac.in/) (joint) at [IIT Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/), where he leads the Computational Design of Functional Materials (CDFM) group.

He received his Bachelor's in Science (Chemistry Hons.) from the [Dept. of Chemistry](https://vbchem.ac.in/), Visva-Bharati University, Shantiniketan, West Bengal in 2009.
He then joined [JNCASR, Bangalore](https://www.jncasr.ac.in/home) as an Integrated Ph.D. student in the [Chemistry and Physics of Materials Unit](https://www.jncasr.ac.in/index.php/research/research-units/chemistry-and-physics-of-materials-unit), completing his Master's in Science in 2012.
He then joined the [Theoretical Sciences Unit](https://www.jncasr.ac.in/index.php/research/research-units/theoretical-sciences-unit) of the same institute for his Ph.D. under the supervision of [Prof. Swapan K Pati](https://www.jncasr.ac.in/faculty/pati), which he completed in 2016.

He then moved to the Depts. of [Physics](https://www.bath.ac.uk/departments/department-of-physics/) and [Chemistry](https://www.bath.ac.uk/departments/department-of-chemistry/), [University of Bath](https://www.bath.ac.uk/), UK, as a postdoctoral research associate for two years, working with [Prof. Saiful Islam](https://people.bath.ac.uk/msi20/) and [Prof. Alison Walker](https://people.bath.ac.uk/pysabw/).
From November 2018 to July 2021, he was a research associate at [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://www.lanl.gov/), USA, mentored by [Dr. Sergei Tretiak](https://cnls.lanl.gov/external/people/Sergei_Tretiak.php) and [Dr. Amanda Neukirch](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=UfX8xC0AAAAJ&hl=en).
He joined IIT Delhi as an Assistant Professor in July 2021 and was promoted to Associate Professor in June 2026.

Outside of research, Dibyajyoti enjoys hiking, playing football, and traveling.
