Dibyajyoti is an Associate Professor in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering and the Department of Chemistry (joint) at IIT Delhi, where he leads the Computational Design of Functional Materials (CDFM) group.

He received his Bachelor’s in Science (Chemistry Hons.) from the Dept. of Chemistry, Visva-Bharati University, Shantiniketan, West Bengal in 2009. He then joined JNCASR, Bangalore as an Integrated Ph.D. student in the Chemistry and Physics of Materials Unit, completing his Master’s in Science in 2012. He then joined the Theoretical Sciences Unit of the same institute for his Ph.D. under the supervision of Prof. Swapan K Pati, which he completed in 2016.

He then moved to the Depts. of Physics and Chemistry, University of Bath, UK, as a postdoctoral research associate for two years, working with Prof. Saiful Islam and Prof. Alison Walker. From November 2018 to July 2021, he was a research associate at Los Alamos National Laboratory, USA, mentored by Dr. Sergei Tretiak and Dr. Amanda Neukirch. He joined IIT Delhi as an Assistant Professor in July 2021 and was promoted to Associate Professor in June 2026.

Outside of research, Dibyajyoti enjoys hiking, playing football, and traveling.
